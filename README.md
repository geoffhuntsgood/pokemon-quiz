# Pokémon Quiz

This app lets you show off your Pokémon knowledge through various quizzes!
Hosted at https://geoffhuntsgood.com/pokemon-quiz.

## Configuration

Choose Pokémon, Moves, or Abilities by generation (1-9), by type (currently 1 of 18), or by various categories.

## Taking the Quiz

Quizzes are timed, and best times will be saved. Type your guesses into the box and they will automatically appear in the list!
